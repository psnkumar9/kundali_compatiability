What is Kundali Milan?

Kundali Milan (Guna Milan) is the traditional Vedic method of assessing compatibility between two persons for marriage or other partnerships. It is based on the Moon's Nakshatra (birth star) and Pada (quarter) at the time of birth.

Ashtakoota System (North India)

Eight factors (Koots) are evaluated, with a maximum of 36 points:

  • Varna (1) — Spiritual compatibility
  • Vashya (2) — Mutual attraction and control
  • Tara / Dina (3) — Birth star compatibility
  • Yoni (4) — Nature, temperament, progeny
  • Graha Maitri (5) — Planetary friendship (Moon lords)
  • Gana (6) — Temperament (Deva / Manushya / Rakshasa)
  • Bhakoot (7) — Moon sign relationship
  • Nadi (8) — Health and progeny (most critical)

Score Interpretation

  • 0–17: Not recommended
  • 18–23: Average — proceed with remedies
  • 24–27: Good — proceed confidently
  • 28–31: Very Good — auspicious match
  • 32–36: Excellent — highly auspicious

South India (Dashakoot)

Tamil / South Indian tradition uses 10 Poruthams (Dina, Gana, Mahendra, Sthree Dheerga, Yoni, Rashi, Raashi Adhipathi, Vasya, Rajju, Vedha). Rajju is considered the most critical and is checked first per Jataka Parijata.

Three Major Doshas

  • Nadi Dosha — Same Nadi (most severe per BPHS)
  • Bhakoot Dosha — Incompatible Moon sign pairs
  • Gana Dosha — Incompatible temperament types

Manglik (Kuja) Dosha

Mars in 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house from Lagna is called Manglik Dosha. If both persons are Manglik, the dosha is cancelled. Various exceptions apply as per BV Raman's commentaries.
